\section{Reading the roadmap}\label{ch13:sec:reading}

This chapter converts the document's decisions into ordered engineering
work on \modrepo{}. Each phase lists its modules (existing, extended, or
new), the chapter that owns the mathematics, and the \emph{gate} that
must pass before the phase is called done. Gates are inherited from the
evaluation constitution of \cref{ch:gof}: every new fitter passes a
parameter-recovery rehearsal on synthetic data generated from its own
family (and a misspecified stress world where one exists), every likelihood
passes analytic-vs-numeric gradient checks at the tolerances already
standard in the repository (\cref{app:results}), and every data
transformation passes its audit. Phases are ordered by the dependency
structure of the mathematics, not by expected impact; the impact
ordering is already encoded in the standing verdicts (covariates first,
risk-set truthfulness above all, excitation last).

\section{Phase 0 --- the real-data spine}\label{ch13:sec:phase0}

\emph{Owns: \cref{ch:data,ch:censoring,ch:universe}. Everything else
blocks on this.}

\begin{enumerate}[label=P0.\arabic*,leftmargin=3.2em]
\item \modrepo{data/pitchbook.py}: the adapter from raw PitchBook
deals/companies/investors extracts (2011--June 2024) to the fitter-ready
arrays of \code{synthetic/loaders.py} --- weekly sector counts, as-of
macro matrix, LOCF firm features \emph{with staleness ages}
(\cref{ch:stale}, D8.1), at-risk masks, event list with universe flags.
The synthetic loader's array contract is frozen as the interface;
\cref{app:columns} is the column contract.
\item \modrepo{data/audits.py}: the defect-ledger validation suite of
\cref{ch:data} --- open-spell retention, entry-date consistency, as-of
join look-ahead test, duplicate-collapse idempotence, coverage-share
report by era (\cref{ch:universe}), date-quality audit separating
announced/closed/backfilled dates (\cref{ch:censoring}).
\item Honest-resolution decision executed: the date audit fixes the
modeling resolution (expected: weekly) per D6.1.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Gate}: audits green on the real extract; a data note recording
coverage share, date-quality composition, and staleness-age
distributions by sector --- the empirical face of
\cref{tab:ch02:defects}.

\section{Phase 1 --- baselines that must be beaten}\label{ch13:sec:phase1}

\emph{Owns: \cref{ch:poisson,ch:buckets,ch:stale} (R1),
\cref{ch:ranking} (linear).}

\begin{enumerate}[label=P1.\arabic*,leftmargin=3.2em]
\item Sector ground baseline: seasonal + macro NHPP via the binned GLM
equivalence (\cref{ch:poisson}), then the negative-binomial noise
upgrade (D4.2); held-out NLL ladder vs.\ random and historical-mean
baselines established as the standing leaderboard (\cref{ch:gof}).
\item \modrepo{panel/}: the bucketed panel builder of \cref{ch:buckets}
--- disjoint buckets per horizon $\horizon \in \{26, 52, 104\}$ weeks,
censoring-aware labels, point-in-time features including staleness ages
and interactions (R1), leakage audits.
\item \modrepo{models/xgb\_horizon.py}: per-horizon XGBoost classifiers
with temporal early stopping and mandatory post-hoc calibration
(D9.5); reliability reports into \modrepo{eval/}.
\item Ranker on real data: the existing linear risk-set ranker
(\modrepo{sector\_ranker.py}) with staleness features, evaluated by the
recall@$K$ protocol of \cref{ch:ranking} --- this is the first real
sourcing-list prototype.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Gate}: the covariates-dominate verdict re-established (or
refuted) on real data --- nats-ladder note comparing seasonal, macro,
and history-augmented sector models; horizon models calibrated within
tolerance; recall@$K$ baseline numbers frozen for all later
comparisons.

\section{Phase 2 --- the committed architecture}\label{ch13:sec:phase2}

\emph{Owns: \cref{ch:hawkes,ch:censoring,ch:ranking,ch:universe}.}

\begin{enumerate}[label=P2.\arabic*,leftmargin=3.2em]
\item Sector-level continuous-time Hawkes at honest resolution via the
MBPP likelihood (\modrepo{mbpp/} already implements the machinery;
wire it to the real arrays); excitation reported with the
upper-bound protocol; the $\branch$--$\decay$ ridge diagnostics of
\cref{ch:gof} standing.
\item Grouped-softmax XGBoost objective (\cref{ch:ranking}'s
gradient/Hessian derivation) so the ranker's score function gains
interactions while keeping the conditional-likelihood family --- the
response to the fired B1 test.
\item Outside option $\lozenge_s$ in the risk set (\cref{ch:universe},
D11.x): newcomer share becomes a calibrated output; recall@$K$
denominators split tracked vs.\ newcomer per protocol; Good--Turing and
coverage audits wired into \modrepo{eval/}.
\item \modrepo{eval/}: the consolidated harness of \cref{ch:gof} ---
NLL ladders, PIT/dispersion, time-rescaling, rolling-origin driver,
recall@$K$/MRR with era-blocked uncertainty --- replacing per-experiment
ad hoc scripts.
\item \modrepo{decision/}: the composition layer of \cref{ch:decision}
(weekly artifacts, caveat propagation, VoI logging).
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Gate}: end-to-end weekly run on real data producing the three
deliverables of \cref{ch:decision}; a full evaluation campaign note in
the style the repository already uses, with every metric attached to
its protocol.

\section{Phase 3 --- escalations, each behind its trigger}\label{ch13:sec:phase3}

\emph{Owns: \cref{ch:stale} (R2--R4), \cref{ch:ranking} (PL),
\cref{ch:universe} (entrants), \cref{ch:hawkes} (signed kernels,
frailty).}

\begin{enumerate}[label=P3.\arabic*,leftmargin=3.2em]
\item \modrepo{covstate/}: the supervised drift dataset from
consecutive-deal pairs and the imputation API (R2), then the OU/Kalman
state layer supplying $\Sigma(\staleage)$ (R3); joint modeling (R4)
strictly behind the attenuation-gap trigger of \cref{ch:stale}.
\item Plackett--Luce likelihood for multi-deal sector-weeks (D10.x);
LambdaMART challenger benchmarked, not adopted, unless it beats the
likelihood family on the consumed metric two campaigns running.
\item \modrepo{entrants/}: cohort entry intensities on the Lexis
diagram (\cref{ch:universe}), unlocking honest sector-total decompositions.
\item Signed continuous-time kernels (route (b) of \cref{ch:hawkes})
and latent-frailty models: only if a consuming question requires a
structural continuous-time inhibition or contagion claim that routes
(a)/(c) cannot express --- each with its own recovery rehearsal first.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Gate}: per item, the trigger that justified it, measured before
and after.

\section{Decision registry}\label{ch13:sec:registry}

The numbered decisions (D$x$.$y$ boxes) of
\cref{ch:question,ch:data,ch:poisson,ch:hawkes,ch:censoring,ch:gof,ch:stale,ch:buckets,ch:ranking,ch:universe,ch:decision}
form the registry against which pull requests are reviewed: a change
that contradicts a decision box must either be rejected or arrive
together with a documented reversal --- trigger fired, evidence
attached, box amended in this document. The document itself is
versioned with the code; \cref{app:results} appends new campaign
verdicts rather than overwriting old ones, so the evidentiary trail
stays auditable.

\begin{codehooks}
New top-level modules by phase: \modrepo{data/} (P0),
\modrepo{panel/} and \modrepo{models/xgb\_horizon.py} (P1),
\modrepo{eval/} and \modrepo{decision/} and the grouped-softmax
objective (P2), \modrepo{covstate/} and \modrepo{entrants/} (P3).
Existing modules extended in place: \modrepo{sector\_ranker.py}
(staleness features, outside option, PL), \modrepo{sector\_stability.py}
(NB noise), \modrepo{mbpp/} (real-data wiring). The synthetic world
(\code{synthetic/}) remains the rehearsal stage for every gate.
\end{codehooks}
