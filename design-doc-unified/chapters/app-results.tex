% app-results — inherited synthetic-rehearsal results

This appendix is the evidentiary record behind every ``inherited
result'' cited in the main text. All numbers come from the repository's
synthetic rehearsal campaigns (\code{experiments/},
\code{EXPERIMENTS.md}, \code{docs/}); none are real-data results. The
transfer convention is fixed here once:

\begin{protocol}[Transfer convention]\label{prot:appres:transfer}
Rehearsal results set \emph{priors and vetoes} for real-data work ---
they tell us which failure modes are live, which parameters are
identifiable at which resolutions, and which designs were falsified in
a world where the truth was known. They never substitute for real-data
evaluation, and no rehearsal number may be quoted externally as a
property of the market. New campaigns append to this appendix; existing
entries are never overwritten (\cref{ch13:sec:registry}).
\end{protocol}

\section*{The rehearsal world}

Twelve sectors; 8{,}000 firms of which 7{,}031 tracked; 520 weeks with
\code{TRAIN\_END} at week 416; $\sim$49k deals; $\sim$27\% newcomer
share overall (23--25\% in test windows, and $\sim$12\% of untracked
deals from untracked \emph{incumbents}); risk sets of $\sim$64
candidates plus the newcomer option. Regime A: well-specified ---
generated by the two-stage weekly model family we fit (26-week cooldown
in the ranker). Regime B: misspecified stress --- a daily firm-level
block Hawkes with self-inhibition (30-day half-life), sector peer
contagion (7-day half-life, true $\decay = \ln 2/7 \approx
0.099$/day), and latent firm quality; the weekly two-stage model is
deliberately not the DGP.

\section*{Reference numbers}

Ranker validation (held-out weeks, 5 seeds; fitted / oracle / random):
top-1 $16.7\%$ / $21.2\%$ / $2.9\%$; top-5 $49.2\%$ / $53.7\%$ /
$14.3\%$; top-10 $69.2\%$ / $71.6\%$ / $28.6\%$; MRR $0.328$ / $0.371$
/ $0.118$ --- the fitted ranker recovers $\sim$80--92\% of the oracle
ceiling, 3--6$\times$ chance. Campaign arms (regime A): event NLL
$3.346$ vs.\ random $4.011$, top-5 $0.446$; regime B: $3.796$ vs.\
$4.172$, top-5 $0.316$. Average sector concentration (ASC) $6.86$ (A),
$4.7$ (B). Numerical hygiene standards inherited by every new fitter:
analytic-vs-numeric gradient agreement $1.3\times 10^{-8}$ (survival),
$1.8\times 10^{-6}$ (block Hawkes), $\sim 10^{-7}$ (event-time);
time-rescaling KS statistics $0.04$--$0.06$ on well-specified fits.

\section*{Recovery and robustness reference numbers}

\emph{Aggregation ($\branch$--$\decay$) ridge campaign} (true
$\decay = \ln 2/7 \approx 0.099$/day): at 1-day bins
$\hat\decay = 0.112 \pm 0.030$ (unbiased); at 28-day bins
$\hat\decay = 0.081 \pm 0.014$ --- biased low $\sim$20\% with a
deceptively tight spread; $\branch$ recovered within $0.28$--$0.38$
around its true $0.35$ at every tested width. \emph{Covariate-noise
campaign (exp22)}: relative Gaussian noise on covariates at fit and
prediction time attenuated fitted choice weights by $\times 0.88$ at
$20\%$ noise ($\times 0.93$ at $15\%$), eroded stage-1 $\bcoef$
correlation from $0.67$ to $0.53$, while prediction was nearly
untouched (covariate lift $\approx 1.0$ nats throughout; top-5
$0.446 \to 0.439$) --- structure erodes before prediction does.
\emph{Overdispersion}: quasi-Poisson dispersion $2.72$ on the regime-B
weekly sector fit (variance $\approx 3\times$ the Poisson prediction).
\emph{Newcomer substream}: fitted separately, the newcomer (immigrant)
stream carried an apparent excitation radius of $0.30$ against
$0.08$--$0.11$ for the repeat-deal stream --- entry waves harbor the
latent common factors, reinforcing the upper-bound rule.
\emph{Newcomer-share calibration}: with the enriched context ASC,
quarterly newcomer-share forecasts met the standing $\pm 2$pp
acceptance target on test quarters.

\begin{warning}\label{warn:appres:context}
Several rehearsal constants are world-specific (cooldown windows,
half-lives, risk-set sizes, newcomer shares) and must be re-estimated,
not assumed, on the 2011--2024 PitchBook data. What transfers is the
\emph{shape} of each verdict --- which defects bind, which parameters
are fragile, which designs were falsified --- per
\cref{prot:appres:transfer}.
\end{warning}
